# DA-ArcNet - Unsupervised Anomalous Sound Detection under Domain Shift

**Author:** Ardhanarieswarar (24I461) | **Team members:** Gokul Kannan (24I463), Jafer Sathik B (24I464), Madhan M (24I466)

**Institution:** PSG College of Technology, Department of Information Technology

**Course:** 23I006 Deep Learning Mini Project | **Framework:** TensorFlow / Keras

Detect faulty industrial machines from sound when only **normal** recordings are available for training and the operating
conditions or noise change at test time (**domain shift**). Datasets: **MIMII DG** (zenodo 6529888) and **ToyADMOS2**
(ToyCar / ToyTrain through the DCASE 2022 development set, zenodo 6355122; the raw ToyADMOS2 record, zenodo 4580270, is 169 GB).

## Method in one paragraph
Log-mel spectrogram crops -> **AdaCMN** (learnable per-mel clip-mean normalisation) -> lightweight separable-conv backbone ->
L2-normalised 128-d embedding -> **ArcFace** classifier over machine section x operating condition + **gradient-reversal domain adversary**
(source vs target). Anomaly score = cosine-kNN distance to the normal training embeddings of the same section (optionally fused with an
autoencoder reconstruction score). Threshold = 90th percentile of a gamma fitted to held-out normal source clips.
Baselines: DCASE 2022 autoencoder, DCASE 2022 MobileNetV2, ArcFace-kNN (all re-implemented here with the same budget).

## Results (full run, 7 machines, DCASE 2022 protocol, official score = harmonic mean of AUC source, AUC target and pAUC)
| Model | AUC source | AUC target | pAUC | Official |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AE baseline (DCASE 2022) | 0.644 | 0.576 | 0.539 | 0.571 |
| MobileNetV2 (DCASE 2022) | 0.566 | 0.582 | 0.541 | 0.547 |
| ArcFace-kNN | **0.760** | 0.658 | 0.567 | 0.628 |
| **DA-ArcNet (proposed)** | 0.745 | **0.665** | **0.571** | **0.632** |
| DA-ArcNet + AE fusion | 0.734 | 0.637 | 0.549 | 0.616 |

Honest reading: DA-ArcNet is **not significantly better** than ArcFace-kNN (+0.004, one-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.35, overlapping bootstrap CIs);
its consistent advantages are a very small model (54 k parameters vs 710 k for MobileNetV2) and slightly better robustness to additive noise.
The gradient-reversal adversary did not make the embedding domain-invariant (domain-probe accuracy 1.00), and the MobileNetV2 baseline did not
train successfully on several machines (chance-level AUC). Full tables are in `results/` and `paper/generated/`.

## Evaluation protocol (DCASE 2022 Task 2)
AUC (source), AUC (target), partial AUC (FPR <= 0.1), official score = harmonic mean; plus EER, AP, accuracy, precision, recall,
specificity, F1, MCC and confusion matrices at the fixed threshold. Statistics: bootstrap 95 % CIs, repeated runs, Wilcoxon tests.
Extras: ablation, cross-dataset transfer (both directions), white/pink noise at 20/10/5/0 dB, Grad-CAM, AE residual maps, t-SNE, model size and latency.

## Notes and honest limitations
* TensorFlow does not use PyTorch's `.pt`; models are saved as `.keras`, `.weights.h5` and SavedModel folders.
* Baselines are re-implementations, not the authors' official systems; numbers are not comparable with challenge leaderboards.
* Thresholds come from source-domain normal clips only; there are only 10 target-domain training clips per section.
* ToyADMOS2 is represented by ToyCar and ToyTrain from the DCASE 2022 development set.
* The thresholds are fitted on source normals, so false-alarm rates on the target domain are high (e.g. ToyCar target: 100 %).
* Multi-seed statistics use three runs on two machines (fan, ToyCar) only.
* The paper PDF in this repository was typeset with a layout emulation of IEEEtran; recompile `paper/main.tex` with the official class before submission.
